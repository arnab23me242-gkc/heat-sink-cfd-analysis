
# Staggered Perforated Fin Heat Sink — CFD Analysis

## Project Overview

This project presents the CAD modelling and numerical analysis of a Staggered Perforated Fin (SPF) heat sink for electronic thermal management.

The study compares a conventional straight-fin heat sink with a modified design containing staggered circular perforations. The objective is to investigate whether the perforated design can improve cooling performance under different operating conditions.

## Objectives

- Design a conventional straight-fin heat sink.
- Develop a staggered perforated fin heat sink.
- Perform CFD simulations using ANSYS Fluent.
- Compare average chip temperatures under different operating conditions.
- Evaluate the thermal improvement of the proposed design.

## Software Used

- SolidWorks
- ANSYS SpaceClaim
- ANSYS Meshing
- ANSYS Fluent 2026 R1 Student Version

## Methodology

1. CAD modelling of the conventional heat sink.
2. Validation of the conventional model against benchmark data.
3. Modification of the design by introducing staggered circular perforations.
4. Computational domain creation and meshing.
5. Application of thermal and flow boundary conditions.
6. CFD simulation under six operating conditions.
7. Comparison of temperature and thermal performance results.

## Results

The proposed SPF heat sink showed lower average chip temperatures than the conventional straight-fin heat sink in all six simulated cases.

| Parameter | Result |
|---|---|
| Minimum temperature reduction | 3.430 K |
| Maximum temperature reduction | 8.244 K |
| Average temperature reduction | 5.966 K |
| Average percentage improvement | 1.90% |

The results indicate that staggered perforations can improve air movement through the fin passages and enhance convective heat transfer.
